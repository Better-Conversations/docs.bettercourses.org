---------
The Board
---------

The Foundation is run by a Board of Directors, who are responsible for the governance of the Foundation. 

- Chandima Dutton, CEO and course owner
- Simon Coles, Chairman and CTO

Board members work closely together, but have formal meetings ("a directors’ meeting") as needed. 

This meets the requirement of the articles that "The directors must ensure that the company keeps a record, in writing, for at least 10 years from the date of the decision recorded, of every unanimous or majority decision taken by the directors".
